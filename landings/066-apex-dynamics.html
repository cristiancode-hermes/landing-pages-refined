Built in three obsessions.

Every decision on the VX-1 lost an argument before it won one. These are the three that mattered most.

01 / The motor

A permanent-magnet axial-flux unit of our own design, oil-cooled, good for 134 kW peak. It makes its maximum torque from zero rpm — which is why there is no clutch, no gearbox, and no forgiveness if you're careless with your right wrist.

02 / The battery

16.8 kWh in a structural pack that is also the spine of the bike. It charges 20–80% in 28 minutes on a DC fast charger — one coffee, not lunch. We publish the degradation curve: 8% capacity loss at 100,000 km, on record.

03 / The chassis

A carbon monocoque with the battery as a stressed member. Titanium subframe. Öhlins at both ends because saving weight and then ruining it with cheap suspension is a joke we've heard before. Dry weight: 218 kg. That's a middleweight with a heavyweight's punch.
