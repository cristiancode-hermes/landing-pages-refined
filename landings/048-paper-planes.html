Hopelessly Picky About Paper

We buy from eleven small mills and binderies — a family shop in Gifu that still sizes its paper with egg white, a Portland letterpress studio that prints on a 1962 Heidelberg, a Barcelona mill making cotton paper from textile offcuts.

Everything in the box gets the same test: our founder Rin writes one full page on it with a wet fountain pen. Feathering, bleed-through, or a scratchy tooth — any of the three and it doesn't ship, no matter how pretty it is.

About 40% of what we sample fails. You're welcome.
