The no-partner promise

Most people arrive alone. Here's why that works.

We rotate, always

Partners switch every two minutes, so nobody's stuck and nobody's left out. By the end of one class you've danced with the whole room.

Leads & follows welcome

Pick either role, whichever appeals — plenty of us learn both. About a third of each class is dancing the role they didn't expect to.

Bring one anyway

Couples are welcome to stay together — just say so at the door and we'll keep you paired. The rotation is an offer, never a rule.

What to put on your feet

The single most-asked question at the door, answered forever.

Yes, perfect

  • Any flat shoe that stays on — trainers are fine
  • Leather soles if you have them (slide is your friend)
  • Keds, brogues, jazz shoes, plimsolls
  • Comfort over glamour, every time

Leave at home

  • Flip-flops and anything backless
  • Stilettos — the floor is 120 years old
  • Grippy running shoes (your knees will complain)
  • Brand-new anything. Break them in first.
