"Nobody has ever had a good day on a river they didn't respect."

  • PFDs stay buckled

    On the water, always, every trip, guides included. It's the one rule with zero exceptions and zero apologies.

  • We turn around

    If a rapid reads wrong on the day, we portage or pull the trip. You'll be annoyed for an hour and paddling for decades.

  • Say the thing

    Scared, cold, tired, blister forming — tell your guide early. Every rescue we've ever run started with someone staying quiet.
