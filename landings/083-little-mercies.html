How adoption works

Four steps, about a week, $85 for dogs and $65 for cats. The fee covers spay/neuter, vaccines, microchip, and a starter bag of whatever they're already eating.

We do a home check — not to judge your baseboards, but because Pickles once got stuck behind a water heater and we'd like that to stay a once.

  1. Come meet them

    Open hours Tuesday–Sunday, 11–5. No appointment needed. Bring the whole household, including the dog you already have.

  2. Talk with a matcher

    Twenty minutes with a volunteer who knows every animal's whole deal. They will talk you out of the wrong animal. That's the job.

  3. Home check & sleepover

    A quick visit, then a three-night trial at your place. If it's not working, no shame and no fee — about 1 in 12 trials end this way and that's fine.

  4. Sign, pay, take them home

    Paperwork takes fifteen minutes. You'll hear from us at two weeks and two months. After that, only if you send photos. Please send photos.

The foster program

Fosters take animals who can't do shelter life — kittens too young, seniors too tired, dogs too scared. We cover food, vet care, and supplies. You cover the couch.

Most fosters last two to six weeks. Some last forever, because the animal never leaves, and we pretend to be surprised every time. There's a badge for it. Forty-one households have earned one.
