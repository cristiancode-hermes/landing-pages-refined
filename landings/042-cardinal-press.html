A small press for short books of poems.

Cardinal Press publishes three chapbooks a year from a two-room studio in Beacon, New York. We print on a Vandercook that is older than our lease, sew the spines by hand, and number every copy in pencil.

No algorithms, no acquisitions committee. Two editors, one red bird, and the conviction that twenty pages is exactly long enough.

Field Guide to Leaving

Pack the good knife. Leave the lamp. A house forgets you room by room — the hallway first, then where you slept, the kettle last, still loyal, warm. Go before the birds come back to audit what you failed to keep.

Low Country Psalm

The tide goes out like an apology and the marsh accepts it, every time. My grandmother hung her grief on nails beside the nets, where it dried silver. Lord of small inlets, teach me that — the leaving, and the coming back.

№ 12 · 24 pages · sewn in red thread · $12

The Cartographer’s Widow

He mapped the coast and missed the house. Every inlet bears another man’s name; our kitchen table holds no legend at all. I fold his charts into paper boats and sail them down the gutter — north, always north, like he went.
