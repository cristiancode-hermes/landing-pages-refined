Paper Moon Radio

Short · 11 min2025Annecy Official Selection

A lighthouse keeper's daughter discovers the beacon doesn't shine light — it broadcasts it, on a frequency only moths and insomniacs can hear. 7,940 drawings. The storm sequence alone took four months and one actual therapist.

It played fourteen festivals and lost to a film about a sandwich at six of them. We'd make it again tomorrow.

Salto!

Series · 8 × 22 min2024Ottawa Grand Prize

Abuela Canela retired from the ring twenty years ago. Then the town's cooking show insulted her pozole, and the mask went back on. Each episode's fight is storyboarded like a recipe: mise en place, then a hurricanrana.

Season two is in clean-up now. The kitchen-fire tag match in episode six is the best 90 seconds we've ever shipped.

Kingfisher

Feature · in production202773,000 frames to go

Our first feature. A kingfisher the size of a weather system migrates once a generation, and a port city has learned to build its year around the bird: festivals when it arrives, silence when it nests, repairs when it leaves.

We're animating the bird on ones because it deserves it. This is either our masterpiece or our cautionary tale. Possibly both.

The twelve, tattooed on the process

Disney's nine old men wrote the principles. We just argue about them daily, over dumplings, at a studio above a bike shop. This is what they mean on our floor.

  • Squash & stretchPriz's face is 40% of our render time and worth every frame.
  • AnticipationIf the audience blinks before the punch, we failed the wind-up.
  • StagingOne idea per shot. Two ideas means two shots. This rule ends meetings early.
  • Straight ahead & pose to poseFights go straight ahead. Acting goes pose to pose. Dumplings settle ties.
  • Follow-throughCapes, hair, and Abuela Canela's skirt have their own department. It's one person. Hi, Soo.
  • Slow in & slow outNothing in nature moves linearly, and neither does anything on our timeline.
  • ArcsEvery limb swings on an arc. Straight lines are for spreadsheets.
  • Secondary actionThe ear flick in the background of shot 212 got a bigger laugh than the joke. We keep receipts.
  • TimingComedy is on twos. Tragedy is on ones. Meetings are on neither.
  • ExaggerationPush the pose until it breaks, then back off exactly one drawing.
  • Solid drawingLife drawing every Tuesday, 8 a.m. Attendance is "voluntary."
  • AppealIf you wouldn't pin the character sheet to your wall, it goes back to the board.
