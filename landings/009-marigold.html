Chef Asha's story

Asha Rangan grew up above her grandmother's mess hall in Madurai, where lunch was eleven dishes on a banana leaf and nobody left hungry — that was the whole business model. She cooked through Lyon and Copenhagen, then came home to the flavours that raised her.

Marigold opened in 2019 with nine tables, one tandoor, and a rule she still keeps: every dish must taste like somewhere specific. No "Indian-inspired." Madurai is Madurai. Kochi is Kochi. The map is the menu.

The marigolds are real, by the way. Her mother strings them fresh every morning and refuses to teach anyone else the knot.

— Asha Rangan

The menu, by region

Three kitchens of India, one dining room. Dishes marked (v) are plant-based, and every curry can be made so. Tell us about allergies — the kitchen keeps a separate pan for you, not a separate attitude.

Cooked over lumpwood charcoal at 480°C. The bread arrives blistered; please don't wait for it to cool.

Two nights in raw-papaya marinade, charred edges, mint chutney with actual bite.

House-pressed paneer, Kashmiri chilli, a yoghurt we smoke over the tandoor coals.

From the Coast — coconut & catch

The fish comes in daily from Brixham. When it's gone, the board says so, and we will not be argued with.

From the South — dosa & fire

The dosa batter ferments for 26 hours. We time the ferment, not the other way round, so dosas stop at 22:00 sharp.

Masala dosa, gunpowder (v) £11.00

Crisp as cellophane, potato masala, milagai podi, three chutneys in descending order of mercy.

Chettinad chicken, stone-ground pepper £14.00

Black pepper ground on stone each morning, star anise, a heat that builds like a good argument.

Filter coffee crème brûlée (v) £7.00

Davara-tumbler coffee under the crack of caramel. The tables applaud it weekly.

The Garland Room

Behind the kitchen is a room that seats sixteen around one long teak table, under a ceiling strung with — you'll never guess. It has its own tandoor, its own playlist, and its own rules: the menu is set by the season, the toast is set by you.

Write to garland@marigold.example with your date. We answer within a day, usually within the hour, occasionally mid-service with flour on the keyboard.
