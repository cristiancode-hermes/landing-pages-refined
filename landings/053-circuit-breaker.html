INCIDENT 0417 · LOGISTICS SECTOR · CONTAINED IN 6 H 12 MIN

At 03:14 on a Tuesday, the night supervisor at a 400-lorry haulage firm in the Midlands noticed the routing servers rebooting in sequence. By 03:51 our on-call engineer had traced the entry to a phishing mail opened nine days earlier by a new starter in the accounts office. The intruder, operating from infrastructure attributed to a ransomware crew tracked as FIN-HOLLOW, was staging encryption across the dispatch cluster. We severed the command channel at 04:40, imaged the compromised hosts, and had routing back on known-good servers before the first lorries loaded at 06:00. Ransom demanded: £1.2M in Bitcoin.
