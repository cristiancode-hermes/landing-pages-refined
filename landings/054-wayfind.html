The dead zone is the point

In March, one of our testers lost signal eleven minutes up Helvellyn and didn't see a bar again for six hours. Nothing went wrong — that's the interesting part. The map kept working, the track kept logging, and the only thing that stopped was the group chat.

That's what Wayfind is for. Not emergencies — though it helps there too — but the ordinary, wonderful fact that the best places have no reception. We build the map for the 94% of the trail your carrier forgot.

Every pack is checked on foot. If a path on our map is wrong, someone on the team walked it wrong first, got wet, and fixed it.
