The best moments have no signal

Sonder is built offline-first, because the entries that matter most happen on night buses, mountain passes and ferry decks. Here's the honest version of what that means:

  • Your journal lives on your device. Every word is written to local storage first. Airplane mode changes nothing.
  • Sync happens when you say so. When you're back on Wi-Fi, entries sync to your account. Conflicts keep both versions — we never silently overwrite.
  • Export is forever, in plain Markdown. Leave tomorrow and take every entry, photo caption and pinned place with you. No ransom, no lock-in.
