A sample week, sailed slowly

Seven days out of Palma, drawn the way we actually sail it — with the weather, not against it. The route below is the chart we hand you on day one.

  1. I.
    Palma — embark at 16:00

    Welcome aboard, safety walk, dinner under the cathedral lights from the bay.

  2. II.
    Palma → Cabrera, 22 nm

    The archipelago is a national park; we hold a mooring permit. Swim where the fish are unbothered.

  3. III.
    Cabrera, at anchor

    A whole day for the blue grotto, the castle walk, and lunch that lasts until four.

  4. IV.
    Cabrera → Sóller, 48 nm

    The long leg. Dolphins on the bow wave more often than not. Sóller's port for the night.

  5. V.
    Sóller → Formentor, 26 nm

    Under the Tramuntana cliffs, anchor off the pine beach by early afternoon.

  6. VI.
    Formentor → Cala Ratjada, 21 nm

    A lazy reach down the north coast, dinner ashore at the fishermen's cooperative.

  7. VII.
    Cala Ratjada → Palma, 52 nm

    Up with the sun, home by evening — or don't. The second week books at a returning-guest rate.
