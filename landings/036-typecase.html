Meridian

Slab serif · 7 weights · 2019, rev. 2024

Meridian began as wayfinding for the Utrecht Science Park — a slab that had to stay legible in rain, at distance, on brushed aluminum. The serifs are unbracketed but softened by a two-degree flare, so signs stopped shouting once they moved indoors.

The 2024 revision added a true light master and redrew the figures; old-style numerals now align with lowercase by default.

Folio Grotesk

Grotesque · 9 weights + italics · 2021

Folio is our answer to the neutral grotesque: one that admits a person drew it. The tails of a and t curl a half-degree more than convention allows, and the G keeps its spur — small decisions that add up to a page that reads warm at text sizes.

Nine weights, true italics drawn on a 9° slant, and a mono-spaced figure set for tables that refuse to line up otherwise.

Cassel

Old-style serif · 5 weights · 2023

Cassel is a book face for people who still read books. Named for the bindery street where its first proofs were sewn, it carries a modest x-height, generous counters, and an italic that slopes like handwriting rather than machinery.

Ships with small caps, four figure styles, and ornaments cut from the bindery's own 1912 brass type — the one revival we allowed ourselves.

Custom work

Half our income is commissions: a weight nobody else draws, a script extension, a wordmark pulled into a full family. Recent work includes a Devanagari companion to Meridian for a Mumbai publisher and a five-width sign system for a Dutch rail operator.

A commission starts with a week of drawing against your actual text — not our specimen sentences — so the first review is already about your voice, not ours.
