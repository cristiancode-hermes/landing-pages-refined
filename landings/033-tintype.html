Wedding photography · est. 2011

Photographs that outlive the day

Fifteen years, two cameras, and a stubborn belief that the best frame of your wedding is the one you never saw being taken.

The unposed philosophy

I will not ask you to tilt your chin, laugh on cue, or walk toward me slowly while holding hands. You have waited years for this day; you should spend it with the people you invited, not performing for my lens.

Instead I work like a quiet guest who happens to hold a camera — close enough for the tear your grandmother hides, far enough that you forget I am there. Couples tell me afterwards they can't remember being photographed at all. That is the review I want.

Eleanor Whitfield Principal photographer — twenty weddings a year, never more
