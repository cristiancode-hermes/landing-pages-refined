feature/01Trace every deploy

CI runs, canary rollouts, feature flags, and rollbacks stitched into a single timeline per service. When support pings "is the new cart live?", the answer is one query, not twenty minutes of tab-hopping across three dashboards.

~340 deploys/week traced on a typical Team workspace

feature/02Alert on what matters

Burn-rate alerts fire when p95 build time drifts 15% over your own 14-day baseline — not when one job hiccups on a busy runner. The flaky-stage detector watches retry patterns and names the exact stage eating your queue.

3 alerts in 30 days on our own repo. All three were real.

feature/03Query like a dev

Every panel in the dashboard is a pipe-syntax query you can open, fork, and save. If you can read a shell pipeline you can read Brightstack — and anything you can chart, you can alert on in two more keystrokes.

median query: 84ms across 13 months of retention
