Live filament:

The materials library, with the strength notes nobody prints on the spool

Every material below has broken on us at least once. The notes say when it will break for you. Prices are per gram, finished part, support removal included.

PLA

The easy one. Crisp detail, forty colors, and brittle as a cracker under side load. Perfect for prototypes and desk toys; wrong for anything that clips, snaps, or lives in a hot car.

$0.06 / g

PETG

Our default recommendation. Tougher than PLA, shrugs off weather, food-safe-ish. Strings like mozzarella if your file has lots of travel moves — we clean that up.

$0.08 / g

ABS

Heat-resistant and sandable, the one for car parts and enclosures. Warps on big flat bases; we print it in the enclosure and sometimes it still loses the argument.

$0.09 / g

TPU 95A

Flexible rubber-ish filament for gaskets, feet, and phone cases. Slow to print, impossible to glue, weirdly satisfying to squeeze. Max part size is smaller than you'd like.

$0.14 / g

Nylon CF

Carbon-fiber nylon for brackets that actually hold weight. Eats nozzles for breakfast (ours, not yours) and drinks humidity from the air — it lives in the dry box until the minute it prints.

$0.22 / g

From STL to doorstep

Three steps, no account required, no spam after. We answer quotes within one business day — usually faster, because Dewi checks the inbox obsessively.

  1. Upload the file

    STL, STEP, or 3MF up to 200MB. Broken meshes get fixed free; impossible geometry gets a kind email explaining why.

  2. Approve the quote

    A human prices it with material options and honest trade-offs. Nothing prints until you say so. Change your mind, pay nothing.

  3. Watch it print

    You get a webcam link to your actual job on an actual printer. Most people check it more often than their email.

The "we failed this print" gallery

Other studios show you perfect benchies. Here's what's on our reject shelf this month — because a shop that hides its spaghetti is a shop that's learning slowly.

The 41-hour lamp shade · ABS

Lifted off the bed at hour 39 like it had somewhere better to be. Now a very avant-garde fruit bowl. We reprinted it in PETG, free.

Cthulhu the planter · TPU

Retraction settings were aspirational. The customer's herb pot grew tentacles. She kept it. We lowered the speed and our blood pressure.

The bridge that wasn't · Nylon CF

A 70mm unsupported span, attempted on a dare. Gravity won in eleven seconds. The reprint with supports holds 18 kilos.
