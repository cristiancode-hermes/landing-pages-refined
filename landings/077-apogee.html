Three flights, honestly reported

Every launch provider shows you the highlight reel. Here is the whole reel, including the frame we'd rather crop.

  • Flight 1

    Nominal. 2024-11-02 · 410 kg to SSO

    First flight, first orbit. Fairing recovery failed when the parafoil tore on splashdown — we fished out the pieces, published the teardown, and flew the redesigned seam on Flight 2.

  • Flight 2

    Anomaly, disclosed. 2025-04-19 · 380 kg to LEO

    Second-stage restart underperformed by 3% due to a sticky helium valve. Payload reached a lower-but-usable orbit; the customer stayed, the valve was redesigned, and the full telemetry is on our site, unedited.

    Nominal. 2026-02-27 · 550 kg to SSO

    Full advertised mass, first fairing refly, bullseye orbit. The mission patch has a small helium valve on it. Ask anyone on the pad crew why — they'll tell you the whole story.
