THIS WEEK'S COVER

The mill gets one more winter

By June Okafor, staff writer · Reporting from the north gate, 6:40 a.m.

The Harrington paper mill will stay open through March, the county confirmed late Tuesday, after eleven hours of talks that ended with a handshake in a parking lot and nobody smiling.

Two hundred and forty-one people work the line at Harrington. Most of them heard the news the way everyone in Hollow Creek hears everything now — from Marla at the diner, who heard it from a foreman, who heard it from a lawyer who would not stop drumming his fingers on the counter.

The county's offer covers wages through winter and nothing beyond it. What happens in April depends on a buyer whose name no one will say out loud, though everyone in town seems to know it.

Where the truck goes

The Paper Run is printed Tuesday night in the basement on Main Street and driven around the county before breakfast on Thursday. If you live on a route, the paper is on your porch by 6:30. If you don't, the post office has it by nine.

Route times slip in snow. We print that in the next issue, with an apology, because that's the deal.
