The sky decides. We take you to meet it.

Small-group glacier and aurora expeditions out of Reykjavík, from September to late March. No buses of forty. No promises the sky can't keep.

Three ways into the cold.

Every expedition runs with a maximum of eight guests and two guides. Crampons, helmets, headlamps and hot chocolate are always included. Doubt is optional.

  • The Blue Cave

    5 hours · 24.900 ISK · Moderate

    A walk into the breathing heart of Vatnajökull. The cave changes every winter; last year's cathedral is this year's tunnel. You see what the glacier built while no one was watching.

    Reserve a place

  • Black Sand & Bergs

    Full day · 31.500 ISK · Easy

    Jökulsárlón lagoon and the diamond beach, timed for the low sun. Thousand-year ice on volcanic sand, and silence you can hear. Lunch is a thermos and a very serious rye bread.

    Reserve a place

  • The Long Night

    Overnight · 89.000 ISK · Demanding

    Two days, one mountain hut, and a sky we refuse to schedule. Snowshoes by day, then we wait — awake, outdoors, and hopeful — for the lights to begin. Sometimes they begin at 21:00. Sometimes at 03:00.

What you actually need.

The cold is honest. Dress for standing still at −10°C, not for the brochure photo.

  • Wool base layers. Cotton kills; it holds sweat against your skin. Wool or nothing.
  • Stiff-soled boots. Crampons need a boot that doesn't fold. We keep rental pairs in sizes 36–48.
  • Two pairs of gloves. One thin for the camera, one thick over them. Cold fingers end aurora nights early.
  • A thermos, if you like. We refill it. The hot chocolate has been described as "structurally important".
  • Leave behind: umbrellas, drones without a permit, and the expectation of a schedule.
