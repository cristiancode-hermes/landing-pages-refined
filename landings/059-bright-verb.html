The speak-first method

Your first class ends with a real conversation. A short, wobbly one — but real.

We don’t start with conjugation tables. We start with the fifty sentences you’ll actually say this week, and we fill the grammar in behind them as you go.

  1. Class 1You order coffee, badly, then well. It counts.
  2. Week 3You argue about something small. Opinion words unlocked.
  3. Week 8You tell a story with a beginning and a punchline.
  4. Month 9B2 exam, if you want the paper. Most people pass.
