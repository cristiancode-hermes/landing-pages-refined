Three vans, quirks included

These are working holiday vans, not showroom queens. Each one is serviced between trips and each one has a personality. We'd rather tell you about it now.

Bindi

2-berth hi-top · 2016 · manual

$95/day

The couples' favourite. Big bed, little kitchen, a fridge that hums you to sleep. Best fuel economy of the fleet at 8.1 L/100km.

Honest quirk The left window needs a firm word to close properly, and the radio picks up exactly one station. It's a good station.

Matilda

4-berth pop-top · 2019 · automatic

$135/day

The family one. Sleeps two adults downstairs and two kids up top, and the pop-top means you can stand up to make the coffee.

Honest quirk The reverse beeper is enthusiastic and the whole campground will know your plans. The awning arm sticks on humid days — lift, don't yank.

Dusty

2-berth off-grid · 2014 · manual

$115/day

Solar panel, second battery, outdoor shower. For the ones who want to free-camp the national-park fringes and not see another van for two days.

Honest quirk 270,000 km on the clock and proud of every one. She rattles at 110. Drive 100 — you're on holiday.

The seven days, planned loosely

Short hops on purpose — the longest drive is under two hours. The plan is a suggestion; the ocean is the itinerary.

  1. Day 1
    Sydney → Kiama · 120 km — pick up by 10am, blowhole by lunch, first swim at Bombo.
  2. Day 2
    Kiama → Jervis Bay · 85 km — Hyams Beach for the white sand cliché. It's true though.
  3. Day 3
    Jervis Bay, parked · 0 km — no driving. Kayak, read, repeat.
  4. Day 4
    Jervis Bay → Batemans Bay · 105 km — pies in Ulladulla, oysters at the shed by 4pm.
  5. Day 5
    Batemans Bay → Narooma · 80 km — Australia Rock at golden hour, seals optional but likely.
  6. Day 6
    Narooma → Merimbula · 110 km — the prettiest leg. Stop at every lookout. All of them.
  7. Day 7
    Merimbula → Eden · 25 km — killer-whale museum, last swim, keys back by 3pm.

What's in the price, and what isn't

No asterisks. Here's the whole list, both columns.

Included

  • Bedding for two — real sheets, not sleeping bags (extra sets $12)
  • Camp chairs, table, cooker & full kitchen kit
  • 300 km per day — the whole route is 480 km total, so relax
  • Insurance with a $500 excess — drop it to $0 for $18/day
  • 24/7 phone-a-human breakdown line

Not included

  • Fuel — budget roughly $40/day at current prices
  • National park entry & campground fees — $12–$45/night depending how fancy
  • The good coffee — we leave a list of the right cafés though
One-way
fees

Explained, not buried

Sydney → Eden along the route: $0 — we need the vans down there anyway. Eden → Sydney (against the flow): $150 flat, because someone has to drive it back up and that someone is our mate Dave. Any other one-way combination: ask, and we'll give you a straight number before you book. That's the whole policy.
