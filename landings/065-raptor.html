Licensed, insured, and boring about it

The exciting part is the footage. The paperwork is deliberately the least exciting thing we do — and the first thing we send you.

  • Certification

    Every pilot, every ticket

    FAA Part 107 and CAA GVC across the crew, night operations and flight-over-people waivers held, renewed before they lapse — never after.

  • Cover

    £5M public liability

    Aviation-specific cover, not a rider on a general policy. Certificates issued to your production office before call time, every job.

  • Airspace

    We file our own NOTAMs

    Controlled airspace, helipad coordination, stadium TFRs — our ops desk handles clearance so your 1st AD never has to think about it.

  • Safety

    A no is a no

    Wind over limits, crowd drift into the flight corridor, a bird that won’t leave — we land and talk it through. The day rate doesn’t change the answer.
