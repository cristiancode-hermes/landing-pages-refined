This month's three

Each pot is weighed, timed, and poured at the table. Water is filtered and held at temperature — never reboiled.

First Flush Darjeeling

2 min 30 · 85°C · $9

Gopaldhara estate, picked the second week of March. Muscatel and green almond; a cup that tastes like the altitude it grew at.

Alishan High Mountain Oolong

3 min · 92°C · $11

Rolled by hand at 1,400 metres in Chiayi County. Lilac, warm milk, a long mineral finish. Good for three infusions, better for four.

Smoked Lapsang, Unsmoked Heart

2 min · 90°C · $8

A Wuyi lapsang dried over fruitwood instead of pine — longan and dried plum first, the smoke arriving quietly at the end.
