We farm the soil. The vegetables farm themselves.

Most farms feed the plant. We feed the ground under it — compost from the village, deep-rooting cover crops over winter, and no digging, ever, because a spade undoes in an hour what the worms built in a year.

It costs us yield in year one and pays it back for twenty. Our beds hold water through August without irrigation, grow fewer weeds each season, and — the part we care about — produce food that tastes like somewhere.

Pickup or delivery — choose honestly

Both get the same box, packed the same afternoon. The difference is price, miles, and whether you get to see the field.

Pickup at the farm gate

Thursdays 3–7 p.m. at the barn on Park Road. Your box is weighed out with your name on the crate, the swap table is open, and Tom is usually around to answer "what do I do with this?" in person.

If you can't make Thursday, a neighbour can collect with your surname — no app, no QR codes, no fuss.

£14.50 / week · small box

Delivery, by e-cargo trike

Fridays within Crediton and Sandford only — that's as far as the trike's battery and our ethics both reach. Boxes arrive in returnable crates that we collect with the next delivery.

We'd rather tell you no than put a diesel van on the road for one salad bag. If you're outside the loop, pickup is the honest option.

£17 / week · small box, delivered

4HOURS A MONTH

The work-share, straight up: give the field four hours a month — weeding, packing, or the Saturday big-dig — and we take £26 off your monthly box. Miss two months in a row and the discount lapses, no guilt trip either way. About a third of our members work their share; the farm genuinely runs on it.
