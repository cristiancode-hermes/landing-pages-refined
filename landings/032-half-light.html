Your negatives, fixed properly.

Half Light is a two-person film lab above the framing shop on Delancey Street. We develop by hand, scan on a real scanner, and tell you the truth about your roll.

We push, we pull, we don't judge.

Shot your Portra 400 at 1600 because the light was dying? Write it on the bag. We'll push it two stops and tell you honestly how it came out. Found a roll of expired Provia in a jacket from 2011? That's our favorite kind of problem.

Every roll gets a name, a log entry, and a temperature-controlled bath — not a slot in a machine that eats sprockets. If something goes wrong in our tanks, we call you before you call us, and the reshoot credit is on the house.
