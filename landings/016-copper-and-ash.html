The room is the point

No TVs, no neon, no playlist louder than a conversation. The lights sit at reading-level on purpose — bright enough to see your company, dim enough to forget your phone.

We keep the door unmarked and the list short. If the stools are full, the host will take your number and the bar will call you when two seats open. It usually takes twenty minutes. It is always worth it.

Walk-ins are the heart of the place; reservations just keep the math honest.

The Tuesday Amaro Club

Every Tuesday at 7 pm, Mara pulls four bitter bottles off the backbar and talks about where they come from — the monastery ones, the alpine ones, the one made by a retired pharmacist in Bologna who answers his email once a season.

No expertise required. Curiosity is sufficient, and the bread is free.
