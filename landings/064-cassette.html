Token math, no funny business

Tokens never expire, the machine never eats one without a fight, and if it does, the bar refunds it from the honesty jar — which has never once been short.

Tournament Tuesdays

Every Tuesday at 7 p.m., one cabinet, $5 entry, winner takes the pot in tokens. The game is drawn from a hat at 6:55 — no practicing, no excuses, no mercy.

First-timers get a bye through round one and a free soda, because everyone remembers their first bracket. House rule: trash talk must be at least 80% affectionate.
