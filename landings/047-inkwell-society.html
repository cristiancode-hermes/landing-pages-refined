We are a small shop on Binder's Lane that sells fountain pens, bottles of ink, and the stubborn belief that handwriting is not dead — only out of practice. Whether you're buying your first pen or your grandfather's needs a new sac, you'll be taken seriously here.

The repair bench

Marisol has restored pens for twenty-two years and answers to "the nib whisperer" only on Saturdays. Bring the pen in, or post it with a letter — she appreciates the symmetry. Most jobs are quoted within a week.

  • Nib tuning & smoothing — from $25. Scratchy, dry, or just not yours anymore.
  • Sac & seal replacement — from $40. Vintage levers and button fillers brought back from the drawer.
  • Full restoration — quoted per pen. Cracked caps, missing clips, sentimental disasters. We have seen worse. Probably.

Letter-writing Sundays

First Sunday of the month, 10am to 1pm. We push the tables together, put out the good paper, the house inks, and a pot of tea, and everyone writes — thank-you notes, apologies, love letters, or just a page to someone who'd never expect one. Stamps are on us. First-timers especially welcome; nobody will read over your shoulder.
