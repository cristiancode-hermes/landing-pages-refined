Eleven Nights in Reseda

Chrome Bumper was tracked in eleven nights, 9 p.m. to 5 a.m., in a rehearsal room between a nail salon and a shuttered RadioShack. The Juno-60 was borrowed from a cousin's wedding band. The LinnDrum was a Craigslist miracle with one dead tom, which you can hear missing on "Neon Lease" if you listen for it.

Mara Voss sang every vocal twice — once close, once across the room — and producer Denny Okafor ran both through the same busted spring reverb, so the record sounds like it's chasing itself down a hallway. That's not a metaphor. That's literally the signal chain.

The rain you hear on "Reseda Rain" is real. Night nine flooded the vocal booth and ruined a shoe, a lyric sheet, and one very good take. The take you hear is take two. Nobody remembers night eleven.
