Saltline is a surf school on the North Sea at Skarvik Point. We teach in 5/4mm suits we provide, in water we'll never lie to you about, with coaches who think February is the best month.

See lesson tiers How cold is cold?

We publish the temperature. Then we get in.

Most schools crop the thermometer out of the photo. We paint it on the door. The North Sea at Skarvik runs from 6°C in March to 15°C in a good September, and every one of those degrees is surfable with the right rubber and the right attitude.

Your fee includes a 5/4mm suit, 7mm boots, gloves, and a hood — rinsed, dried, and warm from the rack. Cold hands end lessons; ours don't end early.
