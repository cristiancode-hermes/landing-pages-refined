Two days in every loaf

Marta started Hearthstone in 2016 with a secondhand deck oven and a starter her grandmother kept alive through three apartments and one divorce.

Nothing here is complicated, and nothing is quick. The schedule below hasn’t changed in nine years, because the bread hasn’t asked it to.

  1. Tue 18:00Mix and first fold — the quiet hour.
  2. Wed 08:00Shape, then into the cold room for a slow proof.
  3. Thu 05:00Oven lit. Scored, steamed, baked dark.
  4. Thu 08:15On the shelf, still singing as it cools.
