Every Friday · June 6 – August 29 · 6pm until the coals give out

Forty stalls. One alley. Eat everything.

Pratt Street closes at five, the lanterns go up at six, and by seven the whole block smells like charcoal, lemongrass, and brown sugar. This is the Night Market — twelve Fridays of skewers, noodles, cold beer, and loud music.

Forty vendors, no repeats, no mercy

Every stall is chosen by people who queue. Here are three the regulars plan their night around — the other thirty-seven you'll have to discover the hard way, which is the fun way.

Char Kings

Pork collar skewers brushed with burnt honey, finished over binchotan. The line forms at 6:04. They sell out by ten — every single week.

Stall 04 · Grill Row

The Dumpling Aunties

Two sisters, one folding table, four hundred pleats an hour. Pork-and-chive is the classic; the black vinegar is not optional.

Stall 11 · Noodle Alley

Mango Sticky

Coconut rice pressed to order, mango cut in front of you, sesame salt on top. Eat it on the curb watching the crowd go by — that's the whole point.

Stall 27 · Sweet Corner

Full vendor list posted at both gates — plus rotating guest stalls each week, so the map you memorized in June is wrong by August.

The grounds, five zones, two gates

Pratt Street between Meridian and the old rail arch. Cash and cards everywhere; the beer tent is card only. Lost? Follow the smoke.

  • Grill RowTwelve stalls of open flame. Bring napkins; leave diets.
  • Noodle AlleyBroths, dumplings, hand-pulled everything. Steaming until midnight.
  • Sweet CornerMango sticky rice, sugar-cane juice, pandan soft serve.
  • The Bar TentSix local taps, one very good lychee highball. Card only.
  • Stage Under the ArchLive sets from 7pm. The rail arch makes everything sound better.
