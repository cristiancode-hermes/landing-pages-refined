2–6 players · 60 min · good first descent

41% of teams escape

A bone-sorter's workshop with a ledger that doesn't add up. Tactile puzzles, real padlocks, one wall that is not a wall. Most teams fail on the last door with ninety seconds left.

The Alchemist's Cell

3–6 players · 60 min · puzzle-dense

Distillation glassware, a cipher in four hands, and ingredients that must be combined in the order the ceiling tells you. Bring someone who reads instructions out loud.

The Flooded Crypt

4–8 players · 60 min · veterans only

Knee-deep sound design (the room stays dry), a rising-water mechanic that physically removes puzzle pieces from reach, and a final lock that has beaten 47 stag parties in a row.

The no-jump-scares policy

Nothing will touch you. Nothing will leap out. Dread doesn't need a hand on your shoulder — it needs a locked door, a ticking lamp, and a clue you walked past four times.

Every room has a lit exit path, a panic button within arm's reach at all times, and a game master watching on camera who will talk you down or walk you out in under a minute. Claustrophobic? Tell us at booking — we'll seat you near the door and keep the lantern bright. Nobody has ever needed it twice.
