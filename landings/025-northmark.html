Our sizing is honest, which means it's a little strange.

Between sizes? Size down.

The Meriden runs long in the toe on purpose — your foot swells at mile twelve, and a boot that fits at 8 a.m. shouldn't cripple you at 4 p.m. The heel stays snug so you don't blister.

Measure in centimeters. Trust the chart.

If vanity sizing has burned you before, skip the guesswork: stand on a piece of paper, trace, measure heel to longest toe, and match it to the chart. Two widths, every size, no upcharge.

Wrong size? We swap it, free.
