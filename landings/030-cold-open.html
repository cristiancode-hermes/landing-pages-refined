Cadence honesty

Most networks promise weekly and ship "whenever." Here's the actual board. If a show slips, we say so in the feed — no filler episodes, no reruns dressed up as new.

Between seasons, feeds go quiet on purpose. Silence is part of the format. You'll get one short tape when a season returns, never a countdown of countdowns.
