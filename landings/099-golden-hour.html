The flight, start to finish

We meet in the dark, an hour before sunrise, at the launch field off Mill Creek Road. You'll watch the envelope come upright in the burner light — most people say this half is worth the price alone.

Sixty to seventy-five minutes aloft, drifting wherever the morning goes. Then the landing, the champagne — a tradition older than our company — and breakfast tacos from Rosita's truck while we pack the balloon.
