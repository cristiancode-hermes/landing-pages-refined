How the editing works

Guide design

Each target locus is screened against a 14-species pangenome before synthesis. Guides with any predicted off-target score above 0.01 are discarded at this stage; about 38% of candidate guides do not survive the filter.

Delivery

Editors are delivered as ribonucleoprotein complexes by biolistic transformation — no integrating vector, no foreign DNA in the final plant. Regeneration runs 11–14 weeks depending on cultivar.

Verification

Every edited line is whole-genome sequenced at 30× coverage. Lines with unintended edits anywhere in the genome are terminated. Our current median is 1.0 intended edit per released line, zero retained off-targets.

Pipeline

Four programs, one stage at a time. We advance a program only when the current stage's pre-registered success criteria are met.
