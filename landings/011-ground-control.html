Skip, pause, or walk away.

No mission lock-in. A subscription should work like a good co-pilot: there when you want it, quiet when you don’t.

  • Skip in two clicks

    Every shipment email has a skip link. No login maze, no retention script, no guilt modal.

  • Pause for a month, or three

    Traveling? Freeze the plan from your account page. It resumes when you say so, not when we do.

  • Cancel without a phone call

    Because it’s coffee, not a gym membership. One confirmation email and you’re done.

  • Stale or late? We re-ship free.

    If a bag misses its window or arrives past its best, tell us once. A replacement goes out with the next roast — you shouldn’t have to file a report.
