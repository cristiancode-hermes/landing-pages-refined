The quote is math first, conversation second

Most solar quotes start with a salesperson on your sofa. Ours starts with three measurements — and if the numbers say your roof isn't worth it, we tell you that instead. About one inquiry in six ends that way, and we consider it a good outcome.

We read your roof from orbit

Satellite and aerial imagery give us pitch, azimuth, shading from trees and chimneys, and usable square meters. No visit, no ladder, no obligation.

We read your last 12 bills

Your actual kWh curve — winter heating, summer cooling, the EV you charge at night — decides the array size. Not the biggest system your roof can hold.

You get the spreadsheet

Panel count, expected yield by month, payback year, and our assumptions in plain cells. Then — only then — an engineer calls to walk you through it.

Panels age. Here's the whole truth about it.

Every panel loses a little output every year. Most brochures mention this in a footnote; we put it in the quote, row by row.

The degradation rate

Our panels lose 0.45% of rated output per year — measured across the fleet, not taken from the datasheet. Year one runs slightly worse at about 1.5%, then settles into the slow decline below.

We warrant 87.4% of day-one output at year 25. If your system dips below that, the panel maker replaces the panel and we do the labor free. It's in the contract, page two, in sentences a person can read.

Install day, hour by hour

A typical 10-panel residential install takes one working day. Here is the whole day, including the parts other companies leave vague.

07:30
Crew arrives, kettle goes on

Two installers, one electrician. Walkthrough of the plan taped to your fridge; scaffold was erected the afternoon before.

08:15
Roof rails go down

Rails are fixed to the rafters, not just the tiles. We photograph every fixing for your file — and for the 25-year warranty.

11:00
Panels racked and wired

Power goes off for about 40 minutes around now. We tell you the exact window at breakfast, not after your router drops.

14:30
Inverter commissioned

The electrician tests string voltages, pairs the monitoring app to your phone, and shows you the live generation dial.

16:00
We leave, roof stays

Site swept, packaging taken away, paperwork — grid notification, warranty, that photo file — in your inbox by evening.
