The rules we render by

Static Bloom is a collective, not a studio. Nobody signs anything. The rules below are the entire contract — they were compiled in 2019 and have never thrown an exception.

  1. RULE_01

    No saved states. A work exists only while it renders. If you loved it, you loved a moment, not a file.

  2. RULE_02

    The seed is public. Every exhibited piece ships with its seed and commit hash. If you can recreate it, you collaborated.

  3. RULE_03

    No neural networks. Noise, fields, L-systems, feedback loops. If a GPU can dream it, we are not interested in its dreams.

  4. RULE_04

    Phosphor over pigment. We are a screen practice. Prints exist only to fund the electricity.

  5. RULE_05

    Delete something at every meeting. The codebase gets smaller or it gets forked. Both have happened.
