The bootcamp is not a vibe.

Six weeks in a Kreuzberg apartment with five PCs, one whiteboard, and a coach who confiscates phones at midnight. No content days. No "mental reset" beach trips. Scrim blocks at 10:00 and 18:00, VOD review until someone falls asleep in the chair.

  • 10:00 — scrim block one, four maps, no excuses for ping
  • 15:00 — demo review; every death gets a reason or a fix
  • 18:00 — scrim block two, vs whoever will actually punish mistakes
  • 23:00 — aim routines; 30 minutes, tracked, graphed, no skipping
