Storytime Saturdays

Every Saturday at ten sharp, Marisol reads three books on the big rug. Voices are done. Roaring is permitted. Grown-ups get the stool circle and the good coffee from next door.

No booking, no tickets, no shushing. If your small person needs to wobble, wobble away — the rug has seen worse.

Yes, touch the books.

We hear it in other shops — "don't touch, please." Not here. Every book in Little Obelisk can be opened, sat with, and road-tested on the rug before anyone spends a penny.

Sticky fingers happen. We keep wipes by the till and a small museum of books that were loved slightly too hard. They are not for sale. They are retired heroes.

Come and find us

Where

3 Bell Yard, off Parchment Lane — look for the red door with the tiny second door in it. The tiny door is for browsers under one metre tall. It opens.

When

Tuesday to Saturday, 9 am – 5:30 pm. Closed Sundays and Mondays for reading. Storytime Saturdays at 10 am, sharpish.

Orders

Any children's book in print, ordered by Thursday, is usually here by Tuesday. We'll wrap it in brown paper and draw a worm on it. The worm is free.
