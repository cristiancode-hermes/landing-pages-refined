The kill-proof promise

If your terrarium dies within a year — for any reason, including honest neglect — bring the jar back and we replant it free. No receipt theater, no interrogation. We'd rather replant a hundred jars than have one person decide they're "not a plant person."

In eight years we've replanted 612 jars. Some people are on their fifth. We know their names and we keep their moss ready.

Every jar ships with a care card

Not a generic one — a card written for your jar, with the names of its actual plants and instructions short enough to fit on a 10 cm square of card stock. If care advice can't fit on the card, the plant doesn't go in the jar. That rule has kept us honest for eight years.

  • Fern: trim any frond that touches the glass.
  • Worried? Bring it in. Looking is always free.

The repotting bar, Saturdays

Every Saturday we drag the big bench to the window and open the repotting bar. Bring your jar — ours or anyone's — and repot it with our moss, our tools, and our unsolicited encouragement. Coffee is included. Staying for three hours is normal.
