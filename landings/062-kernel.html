Small team. Strange games. Shipped anyway.

Kernel is a three-person studio in a spare bedroom in Graz. We make small, odd games about dirt, gravity, and ghosts — and we write down everything that went wrong, in public.

Loam & Lantern

  • Steam
  • itch.io
  • Deck verified

A cozy farming game where the soil remembers what you buried. Plant by day, and decide after dark which of the field's whispers are worth following.

Our first game. Built in 14 months, shipped at 2 a.m. on a Thursday because nobody told us launches happen in the morning.

4,312 COPIES · 94% POSITIVE · 1 DAY-ONE CRASH

Apsis

  • Steam
  • Switch
  • itch.io

A gravity-flip platformer set on a dying space station. Every room is a small argument between you and orbital mechanics, and the station always argues back.

Our second game. 61 levels, 9 of which we still can't beat without the debug menu. The speedrunners found three skips in the first weekend and we kept all of them.

11,907 COPIES · ANY% WR 23:41 · 9 DEV-UNBEATABLE LEVELS
